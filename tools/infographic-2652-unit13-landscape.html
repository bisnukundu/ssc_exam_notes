SSC-2652 • English Second Paper • Writing — 6 marks

💬 A dialogue — questions & answers

Rahim: Hello, Karim! How are you?
Karim: I am fine. What are you reading?
Rahim: A book on great men. Why is reading important?
Karim: Reading gives us knowledge and pleasure. Thank you. See you again!
1
Greetings & opening'Good morning! How are you?'
2
Main discussionQuestions and answers on the topic
3
ClosingA polite ending — 'Thank you. See you again.'
💡

Format: each speaker's words begin on a new line, with the speaker's name and a colon. Keep it short — about 10–12 exchanges. Think of 5–6 questions and answers before you start.

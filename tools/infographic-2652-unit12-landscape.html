Punctuation & Capitalization (Unit-12)

SSC-2652 • English Second Paper

.

Full Stop

End of an assertive sentence.

?

Question Mark

End of a question.

!

Exclamation

After a strong feeling.

,

Comma

Separate items in a list.
'pens, pencils, and books.'

'

Apostrophe

Possession & contraction.
'Rahim's book', 'It's'

“ ”

Quotation Marks

Direct speech.
He said, 'I am going home.'

🔠 Capital letters — use them for:

Beginning of a sentence Proper nouns — Dhaka, Rahim 'I' Days & months — Monday, January Titles — Mr, Dr
💡

Don't mix them up: It's = It is  •  Its = possessive, no apostrophe. Read the sentence aloud — pause where a comma is needed, stop where a full stop is needed.

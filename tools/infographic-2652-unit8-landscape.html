What is a phrase?

A group of words with no subject and no finite verb — so it cannot stand alone as a sentence. A phrase works like a single part of speech.

📦

Noun Phrase

Works like a noun.
'To win the game was his aim.' ('to win the game' is the subject.)

🏷️

Adjective Phrase

Describes a noun.
'A man of honesty is respected.' ('of honesty' describes the man.)

🕒

Adverb Phrase

Tells when, where or how.
'He came in the morning.' ('in the morning' tells when.)

💬 Idioms — fixed phrases with a special meaning

'to kick the bucket'= to die
'a black sheep'= a bad person
'at sixes and sevens'= in confusion

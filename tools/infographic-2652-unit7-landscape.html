Right Form of Verbs (Unit-07)

SSC-2652 • English Second Paper

🔤

'to' + base verb

Modal + base verb too.
'He wants to go.'
'She can swim.'

✅

have / has + past participle

Perfect tense.
'She has finished her work.'

👤

Subject–verb agreement

Singular subject → singular verb.
'He goes.' 'The quality of the mangoes is good.'

📎

Preposition → gerund

After a preposition use verb-ing.
'She is fond of reading.'

⭐ Special structures — use the past form

It is high time …'It is high time we went home.'
I wish …'I wish I were rich.'
as if / as though …Past form after 'as if / as though'.
Each / every / either / neitherTake a singular verb.
💡

How to solve: read the whole sentence first, find the subject and the tense, then choose the verb form. 'He go' is wrong — 'He goes'.

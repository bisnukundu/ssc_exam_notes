Tense (Unit-02)

SSC-2652 • English Second Paper

3 tenses × 4 forms = 12 forms.   Tense shows the time of an action — past, present or future.

Present
IndefiniteSubject + base verb (+s/es)
'He goes to school every day.'
Continuousam / is / are + verb-ing
'She is reading now.'
Perfecthave / has + past participle
'She has finished her work.'
Perfect Continuoushave / has been + verb-ing
'It has been raining since morning.'
Past
IndefiniteSubject + past form of verb
'I saw him yesterday.'
Continuouswas / were + verb-ing
'They were playing when it rained.'
Perfecthad + past participle
'He had finished before I came.'
Perfect Continuoushad been + verb-ing
'She had been waiting for an hour.'
Future
Indefinitewill / shall + base verb
'He will come tomorrow.'
Continuouswill be + verb-ing
'They will be playing then.'
Perfectwill have + past participle
'He will have finished by then.'
Perfect Continuouswill have been + verb-ing
'They will have been working for hours.'
⏰

Time-word clues: 'yesterday' → Past Indefinite  •  'since / for' → Perfect  •  'now / at this moment' → Present Continuous

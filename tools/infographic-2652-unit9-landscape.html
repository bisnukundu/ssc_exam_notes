Tag Question (Unit-09)

SSC-2652 • English Second Paper

✅ Positive statement

'He is honest, isn't he?'

Also: 'They play, don't they?'

⇄
opposite tag

❌ Negative statement

'She does not sing, does she?'

Also: 'They don't play, do they?'

I am → aren't I'I am right, aren't I?' — never 'amn't I'.
Let's → shall we'Let's go out, shall we?'
Imperative → will you'Open the door, will you?'
Use a pronoun'Rahim is here, isn't he?' — never 'isn't Rahim'.
💡

How to make a tag: use the same auxiliary verb as the statement (if there is no auxiliary, use do / does / did by the tense), then change positive to negative — or negative to positive.

Voice (Unit-04)

SSC-2652 • English Second Paper

🟢 Active Voice

The subject does the action.

'He writes a letter.'
➜
be + past participle

🔵 Passive Voice

The object becomes the subject.

'A letter is written by him.'

Assertive

'He writes a letter.' → 'A letter is written by him.'
The object becomes the subject; add 'by + subject'.

Interrogative

'Did he write a letter?' → 'Was a letter written by him?'
The auxiliary verb comes first.

Imperative

'Open the door.' → 'Let the door be opened.'
Use 'Let + object + be + past participle'.

⚠️

The tense never changes in the change of voice. Intransitive verbs have no passive: 'He sleeps.' cannot be changed.

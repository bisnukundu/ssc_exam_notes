Preposition (Unit-11)

SSC-2652 • English Second Paper

🕒 Prepositions of Time

at
A point of time'at 5 o'clock'
on
Days and dates'on Monday', 'on 1 January'
in
Months, years, long periods'in 2005', 'in the morning'

📍 Prepositions of Place

at
A point'at the door'
in
An enclosed place'in the room'
on
A surface'on the table'

📌 Fixed phrases — learn by heart

good at English fond of reading afraid of dogs interested in music
⚠️

'in Monday' → 'on Monday'  •  'good in English' → 'good at English'

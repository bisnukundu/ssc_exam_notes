Paragraphs (Unit-15)

SSC-2652 • English Second Paper • Writing — 8 marks

📝 One paragraph, one main idea — 100–150 words

1. Topic sentenceThe first sentence gives the main idea.
2. Supporting sentencesDetails, examples and reasons — joined with linking words (firstly, moreover, finally).
3. Concluding sentenceEnds the paragraph.

Do not break it into parts — write it as a single paragraph.

UnityOnly one main idea — no unrelated ideas.
CoherenceSentences follow a clear order.
LengthAbout 100–150 words.

Tree Plantation A Moonlit Night Your School Traffic Jam The Value of Time A Rainy Day

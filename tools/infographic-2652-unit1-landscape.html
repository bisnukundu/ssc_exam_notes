Sentence (Unit-01)

SSC-2652 • English Second Paper

Subject + Predicate = Sentence

Rahim (subject) plays football every day (predicate).   A group of words without a finite verb is only a phrase, not a sentence.

📢

Assertive

A statement.
'He is honest.'

❓

Interrogative

A question.
'Are you ready?'

👉

Imperative

Order, request or advice.
'Please sit down.'

🎉

Exclamatory

A strong feeling.
'What a nice day!'

1️⃣ Simple

One clause.
'Birds fly.'

2️⃣ Complex

One main clause + subordinate clause(s).
'I know that he is honest.'

3️⃣ Compound

Two main clauses joined by and / but / or.
'He came and sat down.'

💡

How to find the subject: first find the finite verb, then ask 'who / what + verb?'. A phrase has no subject and no finite verb.

Speech / Narration (Unit-05)

SSC-2652 • English Second Paper

💬 Direct Speech

The speaker's exact words inside quotation marks.

He said, 'I am ill.'
➜

📝 Indirect Speech

The meaning reported — no quotation marks. Tense moves one step back.

He said that he was ill.

Assertive

Use 'that'.
'He said, "I am ill."' → 'He said that he was ill.'

Interrogative

'if' for yes/no; wh-word for wh-questions.
'Where do you live?' → 'He asked me where I lived.'

Imperative

ordered / requested / advised + to-infinitive.
'Please help me.' → 'He requested me to help him.'

Exclamatory

exclaimed with joy / sorrow.
'Hurrah! We have won.' → 'She exclaimed with joy that they had won.'

🕒

Time words change too: today → that day  •  tomorrow → the next day  •  yesterday → the previous day

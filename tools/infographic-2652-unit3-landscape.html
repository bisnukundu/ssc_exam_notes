Infinitive, Gerund, Participle (Unit-03)

SSC-2652 • English Second Paper

➡️

Infinitive

to + base verb
  • As a noun: 'To err is human.'
  • As an adjective: 'I have a lot of work to do.'
  • To show purpose: 'He came to see me.'
🔄

Gerund

verb + ing (as a noun)
  • As subject: 'Swimming is good exercise.'
  • As object: 'She likes reading.'
  • After a preposition: 'He is fond of playing.'
🏷️

Participle

verb + ing / past participle
  • Present Participle: 'a barking dog' (verb + ing as an adjective)
  • Past Participle: 'a broken window' (verb + ed / en as an adjective)
  • Non-finite verbs do not change with the subject.
⚠️

Remember: after a preposition always use the gerund ('fond of reading'). After 'to' use the base verb — except 'used to', 'accustomed to' and 'look forward to', where the gerund is used.

Linking Words / Connectors (Unit-10)

SSC-2652 • English Second Paper

🔗

Coordinators

Join two equal parts

  • and — addition
  • but — contrast  'He is poor but honest.'
  • or — choice
  • so — result
🌉

Subordinators

Join a dependent clause to a main clause

  • because — reason
  • although — contrast  'Although it was raining, we went out.'
  • when — time  •  if — condition
  • since — time / reason
➡️

Transitions

Join two sentences or paragraphs

  • however — contrast  'He worked hard; however, he failed.'
  • therefore — result
  • moreover — addition
  • firstly / finally — order
⚠️

Never 'but' after 'although': 'Although he is poor but he is honest' is wrong — use only one. First find the relation: addition, contrast, reason or result — then choose the word.

১০Unit 10 — Linking words or Connectors (p. 101–110)Unit
সহজ ব্যাখ্যা:
Linking words, or connectors, join words, sentences and ideas together and show the relation between them. (Linking words = বাক্য ও ভাব জোড়া দেওয়ার শব্দ।) Without them, writing becomes a list of separate sentences. There are three main groups: coordinators join two equal parts, subordinators join a dependent clause to a main clause, and transitions join two sentences or paragraphs.

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ পয়েন্ট:
• Coordinators: and (addition), but (contrast), or (choice), so (result) — 'He is poor but honest.'
• Subordinators: because (reason), although (contrast), when (time), if (condition), since (time/reason) — 'Although it was raining, we went out.'
• Transitions: however (contrast), therefore (result), moreover (addition), firstly/finally (order) — 'He worked hard; however, he failed.'

পরীক্ষার প্রস্তুতি:
Connector questions ask you to fill a gap with the right linking word. First read the whole sentence and find the relation: addition, contrast, reason or result. Common mistakes: using 'but' after 'although' ('Although he is poor but he is honest' is wrong — use only one), and using 'and' where a contrast word is needed.

Quick Revision:
Coordinators: and, but, or, so. Subordinators: because, although, when, if, since. Transitions: however, therefore, moreover, firstly, finally.

১৫Unit 15 — Paragraphs (p. 171–178)Unit
সহজ ব্যাখ্যা:
A paragraph is a short piece of writing on one main idea, written in about 100–150 words. (Paragraph = অনুচ্ছেদ।) It has three parts: the topic sentence (the first sentence, which gives the main idea), the supporting sentences (details, examples and reasons), and the concluding sentence (which ends the paragraph). A paragraph carries 8 marks in the Writing part.

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ পয়েন্ট:
• Unity: a paragraph must have only one main idea — do not add unrelated ideas.
• Coherence: the sentences must follow a clear order and be joined with linking words (firstly, moreover, finally).
• Common paragraph topics: Tree Plantation, A Moonlit Night, Your School, Traffic Jam, The Value of Time, A Rainy Day.
• Length: about 100–150 words, written as a single paragraph — do not break it into parts.

পরীক্ষার প্রস্তুতি:
Before writing, note down 5–6 points on the topic, then arrange them in order: introduction, benefits or details, and conclusion. Common mistakes: writing many ideas in one paragraph, writing a paragraph that is too long, and forgetting the concluding sentence. Practise one paragraph every week from the book's list.

Quick Revision:
One main idea only. Topic sentence → supporting sentences → concluding sentence. 100–150 words, one paragraph. 8 marks.

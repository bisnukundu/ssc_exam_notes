৪Unit 4 — Voice (p. 45–52)Unit
সহজ ব্যাখ্যা:
Voice shows whether the subject does the action or receives it. (Voice = কর্তা কাজ করে, নাকি কাজ কর্তার উপর হয়।) In the Active Voice the subject does the action ('He writes a letter.'). In the Passive Voice the object becomes the subject and the verb takes the form 'be + past participle' ('A letter is written by him.'). The tense never changes in the change of voice.

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ পয়েন্ট:
• Assertive: Active 'He writes a letter.' → Passive 'A letter is written by him.' The object becomes the subject, the verb becomes 'be + past participle', and 'by + subject' is added.
• Interrogative: 'Did he write a letter?' → 'Was a letter written by him?' The auxiliary verb comes first.
• Imperative: 'Open the door.' → 'Let the door be opened.'
• Some verbs have no passive: intransitive verbs ('He sleeps.') cannot be changed into the passive voice.

পরীক্ষার প্রস্তুতি:
Voice change is a regular Grammar item (5 marks). First find the tense of the active sentence, then use the same tense in the passive. Common mistakes: changing the tense by accident, and forgetting 'be' before the past participle. Practise five assertive, three interrogative and three imperative sentences every day.

Quick Revision:
Active: subject does the action. Passive: be + past participle. Tense never changes. Intransitive verbs have no passive.

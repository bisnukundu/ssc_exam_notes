৫Unit 5 — Speech / Narration (p. 53–62)Unit
সহজ ব্যাখ্যা:
Narration is reporting what someone said. (Narration = কারো কথা বর্ণনা করা।) In Direct Speech we use the speaker's exact words inside quotation marks: He said, 'I am ill.' In Indirect Speech we report the meaning without quotation marks: He said that he was ill. When we change direct into indirect speech, the tense usually moves one step back (present → past) and pronouns and time words change.

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ পয়েন্ট:
• Assertive: 'He said, "I am ill."' → 'He said that he was ill.' The conjunction 'that' is used.
• Interrogative: 'He said, "Where do you live?"' → 'He asked me where I lived.' Use 'if' for yes/no questions and the wh-word for wh-questions.
• Imperative: 'He said, "Please help me."' → 'He requested me to help him.' Use 'ordered/requested/advised + object + to-infinitive'.
• Exclamatory: 'She said, "Hurrah! We have won."' → 'She exclaimed with joy that they had won.'

পরীক্ষার প্রস্তুতি:
Narration is a regular Grammar item. First find the kind of the reported sentence (assertive, interrogative, imperative or exclamatory), then apply its rule. Common mistakes: forgetting to change the tense, and forgetting to change time words — 'today' becomes 'that day', 'tomorrow' becomes 'the next day', 'yesterday' becomes 'the previous day'.

Quick Revision:
Direct = exact words in quotes. Indirect = reported meaning. Tense moves one step back. today→that day, tomorrow→the next day.

❓ MCQ — অনুশীলনী৪টি প্রশ্ন
১. Choose the indirect form: He said, 'I am ill.'
He said that he is ill.He said that he was ill.He said that I am ill.He said that he had been ill.
✅ সঠিক উত্তর: He said that he was ill.
২. Choose the indirect form: He said to me, 'Where do you live?'
He asked me where do I live.He asked me where I lived.He asked me where did I live.He asked me where I live.
✅ সঠিক উত্তর: He asked me where I lived.
৩. Choose the indirect form: He said, 'Please help me.'
He ordered me to help him.He requested me to help him.He asked me help him.He said me to help him.
✅ সঠিক উত্তর: He requested me to help him.
৪. Choose the indirect form: She said, 'Hurrah! We have won.'
She exclaimed with joy that they had won.She said that they have won.She exclaimed with joy that they win.She said that we have won.
✅ সঠিক উত্তর: She exclaimed with joy that they had won.

৯Unit 9 — Tag question (p. 93–100)Unit
সহজ ব্যাখ্যা:
A tag question is a short question added at the end of a statement to ask for agreement or confirmation. (Tag question = বাক্যের শেষে জুড়ে দেওয়া ছোট প্রশ্ন।) Example: 'She is happy, isn't she?' The tag is made with the same auxiliary verb as the statement, a pronoun for the subject, and the opposite form — a positive statement takes a negative tag, and a negative statement takes a positive tag.

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ পয়েন্ট:
• Positive statement → negative tag: 'He is honest, isn't he?' 'They play, don't they?'
• Negative statement → positive tag: 'She does not sing, does she?' 'They don't play, do they?'
• Special rules: 'I am right, aren't I?' (never 'amn't I'); 'Let's go out, shall we?'; Imperative → 'will you': 'Open the door, will you?'
• The pronoun in the tag is the pronoun form of the subject: 'Rahim is here, isn't he?'

পরীক্ষার প্রস্তুতি:
Tag questions are a regular Grammar item. First find the auxiliary verb of the statement (if there is no auxiliary, use do/does/did according to the tense), then change positive to negative or negative to positive. Common mistakes: using the noun instead of the pronoun in the tag ('isn't Rahim' is wrong), and forgetting 'aren't I' after 'I am'.

Quick Revision:
Positive → negative tag; negative → positive tag. Same auxiliary + pronoun subject. I am → aren't I. Let's → shall we.

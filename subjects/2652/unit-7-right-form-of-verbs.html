৭Unit 7 — Right form of verbs (p. 73–82)Unit
সহজ ব্যাখ্যা:
The 'right form of verbs' means using the correct form of a verb according to the tense, the subject and the structure of the sentence. (সঠিক verb-form ব্যবহার করাই এই Unit-এর বিষয়।) The verb changes with time (tense), with the subject (subject-verb agreement) and with the words before it — for example, 'to' is followed by the base verb, and a modal (can, could, will, should) is also followed by the base verb.

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ পয়েন্ট:
• 'to + base verb': 'He wants to go.' 'have/has + past participle': 'She has finished her work.'
• Subject-verb agreement: a singular subject takes a singular verb — 'He goes', 'The quality of the mangoes is good.' 'Each', 'every', 'either', 'neither' take a singular verb.
• Special structures: 'It is high time we went home.' (past form after 'it is high time'); 'I wish I were rich.'; 'as if/as though' takes the past form.
• After a preposition, use the gerund: 'She is fond of reading.'

পরীক্ষার প্রস্তুতি:
This is one of the most important Grammar items. Read the whole sentence first, find the subject and the tense, and then choose the verb form. Common mistakes: forgetting the 's/es' with he/she/it in the present tense ('He go' is wrong), and using the base verb after 'has/have'. Practise ten sentences every day from the book.

Quick Revision:
'to' + base verb; modal + base verb; have/has + past participle. Singular subject → singular verb. After a preposition → gerund.

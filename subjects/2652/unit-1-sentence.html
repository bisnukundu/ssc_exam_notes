১Unit 1 — Sentence (p. 1–18)Unit
সহজ ব্যাখ্যা:
A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought. (বাক্য = সম্পূর্ণ ভাব প্রকাশ করে এমন শব্দগুচ্ছ।) Every sentence has two parts: the Subject — who or what the sentence is about — and the Predicate — what is said about the subject. Example: 'Rahim (subject) plays football every day (predicate).' A group of words without a finite verb is not a sentence; it is only a phrase.

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ পয়েন্ট:
• Four functions of sentences: (1) Assertive — a statement ('He is honest.'); (2) Interrogative — a question ('Are you ready?'); (3) Imperative — an order, request or advice ('Please sit down.'); (4) Exclamatory — a strong feeling ('What a nice day!').
• Three structures: (1) Simple — one clause ('Birds fly.'); (2) Complex — one main clause + one or more subordinate clauses ('I know that he is honest.'); (3) Compound — two main clauses joined by and/but/or ('He came and sat down.').

পরীক্ষার প্রস্তুতি:
In the Grammar part (12 questions × 5 = 60 marks), sentence identification is a common item. First find the finite verb, then ask 'who/what + verb?' to find the subject. Common mistake: calling a phrase a sentence — a phrase has no subject and no finite verb. Practise changing one sentence into the other three functions.

Quick Revision:
Subject + Predicate = Sentence. Functions: Assertive, Interrogative, Imperative, Exclamatory. Structures: Simple, Complex, Compound.
